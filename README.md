# NEXTPHASE
A hackathon repository regarding the "AI powered personal health copilot ."


CureBuddy is an AI-powered healthcare assistant designed to simplify and organize an individual's healthcare journey.
It analyzes medical records, prescriptions, diagnostic reports, and wellness data to provide meaningful insights.
The platform uses AI and OCR to extract important information from uploaded medical documents.
It converts complex medical terminology into easy-to-understand summaries and explanations.
Users can track health records over time, visualize trends, and access organized medical information in one place.
The copilot enables users to ask questions about their records and prepares relevant questions for healthcare professionals.
With a focus on data privacy, transparency, and safety, it supports informed healthcare decisions without replacing medical professionals.
Our mission is to make healthcare information more accessible, understandable, and manageable through intelligent AI technology.
